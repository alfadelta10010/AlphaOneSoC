Three independent passes: RTL compliance and hazards (riscv-design-reviewer), PPA and claims (riscv-design-reviewer, efficiency pass), and a verification audit. The lead added housekeeping and spot-checked the key evidence. Area is in TinyTapeout tiles: 1 t ≈ 11,300 µm² of std-cell area at 60% density; 6x2 = 12.35 t, 8x2 = 16.52 t.

This branch exists to find and rank area cuts. Whether the SoC fits a tile shape and meets timing is tested in the TinyTapeout submission repository with LibreLane.

Verdict

The RTL is correct and the speed-up is real. The area cut is real in relative terms, about −24%. The tile figures, the "81% of 6x2" claim and the timing claim are wrong. No TinyTapeout harden completes, on this branch or on main. The docs and the run.sh X blind spot are now fixed on the branch. The harden failure moves to the submission repository, where cuts are hardened; this branch improves it (fewer hold buffers) but does not solve it.

  • Add nf_misa_c.S (from the compliance pass's probes/) as a spike test at +IMEM_WAIT=1 and +IMEM_WAIT=2.

  • Add a flash-boot SoC test that runs through boot_flash.S with +RF_X.
  • Have the regression audit flag riscv-dv tests that retire under ~5% of their program.
  • Add wait-state lines for rs2_snoop and pmp_refetch, and a positive control for mtip_stale (b).
  • Add an assertion that no fetch completes while EX holds an ex_nofetch instruction.
  • Re-baseline area with the TT cell set.
  • Harden in the submission repository before any tile-shape claim.

What held up

  • ex_nofetch uses only IF/EX decode, which changes on !stall edges, so imem_req never drops mid-fetch.
  • The refetch target is the correct fall-through after compressed, straddling and rs2-phase code.
  • Malformed SYSTEM words, U-mode MRET, and ECALL against an interrupt are all legal and match Spike.
  • No control flop lacks a reset, there are no latches or combinational loops, and no construct mismatches between simulation and synthesis.
  • 3,005 + 2,020 fresh lockstep runs, 0 mismatches, with about 218k interrupt-handler entries and 85 runs through the whole SoC.
  • Every contract-breaking ex_nofetch mutant is killed by the handshake checkers or a trace mismatch.
  • All suites reproduce, with and without +RF_X, and all 75 references regenerate byte-identical from Spike.
  • The five review probes still kill their mutants after ef9037d.
  • No unit-bench check was weakened. The shift checks moved to tb_muldiv rather than being dropped.
  • The river_core numbers and labels are correct, and the run used the tip RTL.
  • The relative area cut and every performance number reproduce.
